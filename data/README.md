# data

`expected_aggregates.tsv` is a frozen snapshot of the registration aggregates built by [LUOaini1213/malaysia-auto-ask](https://github.com/LUOaini1213/malaysia-auto-ask) (file `warehouse/serving/inputs/expected_aggregates.tsv`, commit `9b7faff`). It covers car registrations from January 2025 to August 2026.

The aggregates are derived from the Road Transport Department (JPJ) vehicle registration transactions published on Malaysia's open data portal: [data.gov.my – registration_transactions_car](https://data.gov.my/data-catalogue/registration_transactions_car). The catalogue page states: "This data is made open under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)." Data sources named there: Road Transport Department and Ministry of Transport, Malaysia.

Attribution: contains data from data.gov.my (Road Transport Department / Ministry of Transport, Malaysia), CC BY 4.0. The figures here are aggregated and transformed; registration counts are not sales figures.
